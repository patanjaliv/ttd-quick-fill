# Privacy policy — TTD Quick Fill

- The extension stores the pilgrim and contact details you enter in your
  browser's extension storage (`storage.local`) on your device only.
- It runs only on `ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in` and `tirupatibalaji.ap.gov.in`,
  and only writes your saved details into the booking form you have open.
  Submitting that form to TTD is done by you, not by the extension.
- You can optionally protect those details with a passphrase; they are then
  stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and decrypted only on your device after you
  enter the passphrase. The passphrase is never stored.
- It has no server, makes no network requests of its own, and collects no
  analytics, telemetry or crash reports.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes the stored data.
